//Make Your Mug Happy//

The Koffi ritual

Three steps. None of them fast.

1

Pick your blend

Choose the pace you want your day to take. There are no wrong answers, only rushed ones.

2

Brew it slow

Four minutes of doing absolutely nothing else. The steam needs somewhere to go — so do your thoughts.

3

Sip with space

Find the biggest room in the house. Sit near the door if it helps. Breathe.
